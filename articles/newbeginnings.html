New beginnings

16 December 2025

I was reading the almanack of naval ravikant and there was a section that stuck with me. Naval talked about how once a person betrayed his psyche enough times, there would be a permanent kind of damage done to the soul.

I am spiritual in that way. I believe that every action you do that you know is wrong and feel some kind of shame towards is not a zero sum forgivable action.

It is the law of the universe and the law of equal exchange. For everything you want to have and everything you do have, you are giving up having something else.

I think a secret to solving this law of equivalent exchange in the universe is being very quick to sticking to decisions. It is spending time , a lot of time contemplating about what you really want out of your life. And then ruthlessly denying everything else that is not that.

If you make a plan there is a chance you won't get there. But if you have no plan at all then you definitely aren't getting there. You could get there if you are lucky but there is no fun in that.

You have to begin with spiritually cleaning out the debts you've made to the universe. Every unclosed loop you have. The debts you have in the material world, monetary debts from student loans, or from credit cards. Ending elationships you silently resent, or attwempting to reconcile relationships you miss. It is quitting the habits that you know are bad for your soul. Quitting the bad habits should come before you try to add good ones. Quit sugar, quit obesity, quit partying, quit degeneracy, quit participating in the seven deadly sins and quit living without any virtue. Eventhough you may forget your action, the act of forgetting doesn't erase the action from your eternal psyche.

The first step to improvement is accepting that you made some wrong shameful decisions in your past which is extremely difficult to do. It will take 18-24 months of intentional actions at the most to completely remove all the spiritual debt you have accumulated in your life time.

Once you clear your psyche and find peace for the first time, you will pat your back and understand this was all your doing. You will never ever want to be in a position like that again.
