A fire is a fire

14 December 2025

I am wandering through the icy storm. My ears are ringing from the cold and my fingers have lost any sensation 15 miles ago. I am cold. I am looking for a fire. The only thing on my mind is fire. All my senses beyond my primal desire have been functionally forgotten and unusable. Scanning for any sign of civilization, of a light peeking through the cold snow.

But then i see it. A cave. It is a small cave that is embedded in the side of a large mountain i come across. I come and enter the cave and their is no longer any snow falling onto me. I feel eternally grateful. I know that I wont die tonight. I can stay here and rest until the storm is over.

When your inner world is in shambles, like that of a cold storm. When you find a cave that is an insult to the telos of shelter. THe ideal shelter is a loving home with a comfortable bed. Access to food and water. A place where you can be calm and it is safe without a doubt.

When i arrived into this shelter i prayed to the gods; this was the best thing to happen to me in my life. I was about to die tonight.

The next day

the storm has blown over. I feel the warmth of the sun. Because of my eternal gratefulness to the gods, i promise to never leave, i worship this cave from now on.
For it has rescued me from death.
I die at the footing of the cave three decades later, i die happy.
